      Market confirmation detail

      How to read these measures
      MAG7 share
      MAG7’s contribution as a share of total index movement, counting both directions. 90% means nearly everything the index did came from seven stocks.
      Driving / Offsetting
      Driving: MAG7 moved with the index. Offsetting: MAG7 pulled against it, and the rest of the market moved the index anyway.
      Healthy leadership
      How many of the seven are in Leader or Accumulation state. Fewer leaders carrying a big share means a narrower, more fragile advance.
      SPX Health
      Composite measure of internal support beneath the index. Higher is healthier; below 50 is fragile, below 30 broken.
      Index Risk
      Aggregate structural exposure the index is carrying. Higher means more of its level depends on a narrow base.
      Structural risk
      Concentration and dependency inside the leadership set.
      Divergence risk
      How far price and leadership internals disagree. At 60 and above the engine treats transmission as broken.
      Funding pressure
      Capital being rotated out of some constituents to pay for positions in others.
      Leadership diffusion (LDI)
      Whether leadership is spreading across the seven or narrowing. Negative means narrowing.
      Ex-MAG7 breadth
      Share of the other 493 S&P stocks that advanced. Near or below 50% while the index rises is a sign the move is not broadly confirmed.
      Cap vs equal weight
      Equal-weight return minus cap-weight return. Positive means the average stock did better than the index; negative means mega-caps carried it.
